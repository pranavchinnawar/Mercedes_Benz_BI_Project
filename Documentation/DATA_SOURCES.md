## 4. Data Preparation 🧹

The report's data model contains financial tables, year and quarter dimensions, geographical sales data, and country-to-region mappings.

Data was organized for use in Power BI, with calculations and visualizations based on the fields and measures included in the report.

The actual source, transformation steps, and calculation definitions should be documented alongside the relevant dataset where possible.

## 5. Important Limitations ⚠️

Mercedes-Benz's 2022 sales announcement reports customer deliveries, whereas subsequent reporting uses Group sales figures. Consequently, some geographical sales figures may not be directly comparable across the reporting periods.

Financial and sales values should be interpreted using the definitions and units specified in the corresponding official publications.
